# Vally Ski / Valentina Bernardi

## Register
Brand. This is the existing public, multilingual site for ski instructor Valentina Bernardi.

## Users and purpose
Visitors compare private, children's and family lessons in Les Arcs, Val d'Isère and Tignes, then request availability through the contact form, email or WhatsApp. Italian, English and French are equally supported. The site states that rates are provided in reply and exclude lift passes and equipment rental.

## Existing identity and direction
Preserve the personal, patient and enthusiastic voice expressed in the About section. Preserve the name, photography, Bodoni Moda and Jost pairing, and the charcoal and pink palette taken from the instructor's uniform. This pass refines the existing identity rather than establishing a new brand.

## Principles for this refinement
- Keep lesson choices, contact options and conditions easy to find.
- Make the site readable on a phone, including outdoors where light is bright.
- Preserve dates, qualifications, experience calculation, quoted reviews, prices and business details.
- Support keyboard navigation, reduced motion, zoom and clear form labels.
- Avoid generic stock imagery, invented testimonials, new promises or a replacement visual identity.

Source: existing components, configuration, translations and stylesheet; user mandate to improve the existing site.
